# Equity Lawyer Douyin Round 2 Research Design

## Goal

Identify the repeatable content-growth engine for a new equity, investment, and shareholder-dispute lawyer IP on Douyin.

Round 2 prioritizes audience growth. Commercial conversion, legal-service packaging, and lead quality are not primary ranking factors.

## Decisions This Research Must Support

1. Which topics repeatedly generate high likes, saves, shares, and comments?
2. Which viral structures work across multiple independent authors?
3. Which formats can the lawyer produce consistently?
4. Which three to five content pillars should launch the account?

## Research Strategy

Use a combined strategy rather than equal keyword expansion or head-author copying:

- 60% proven high-spread topics;
- 25% high-performing author backtracking;
- 15% adjacent-topic exploration and low-performance control samples.

No application feature development is included. Existing MXNZP search, author expansion, transcript extraction, comment retrieval, database Run/Candidate records, and Markdown documentation are sufficient.

## Author Backtracking

Compare three author types:

- 40% lawyers;
- 40% equity consultants and business advisors;
- 20% general business-story or knowledge accounts.

Initial author candidates:

- 宋老师专注股权
- 宋老师股权指导
- 王亮聊股权
- 潘律商业律师
- 李晨文公司法
- 韩玲律师讲股权
- 吕飞税务律师

Resolve author identity before expansion because similar display names may represent different people or matrix accounts.

For each selected author:

1. Retrieve high-like works.
2. Retain 8–12 relevant works.
3. Measure repeated performance instead of only the author's top work.
4. Identify recurring hooks, formats, themes, durations, and calls to action.
5. Compare lawyer and non-lawyer treatment of the same topic.

An author becomes a primary benchmark when at least one condition holds:

- three relevant works exceed 10,000 likes;
- five works substantially outperform the author's normal range;
- one content model is repeatedly successful;
- saves or shares remain unusually high across multiple related works.

## Viral Evaluation

Record for each candidate:

- likes, saves, comments, and shares;
- publish date and duration;
- author and follower count when available;
- performance relative to the author's normal range;
- matched research keyword;
- opening hook;
- conflict roles;
- concrete loss;
- number use;
- case use;
- dialogue or scene use;
- number of proposed solutions;
- ending pattern.

Absolute tiers:

- S: at least 100,000 likes;
- A: at least 10,000 likes;
- B: at least 3,000 likes.

Relative-breakout and high-potential samples remain eligible when:

- the work substantially exceeds the author's normal performance;
- saves approach or exceed likes;
- shares are unusually high;
- comments contain many similar lived experiences;
- the same structure succeeds across independent authors.

Commercial conversion does not increase a sample's Round 2 rank.

## Transcript Analysis

Analyze 50–60 complete transcripts using the same dimensions:

1. Hook type: counterintuitive claim, loss warning, relationship conflict, direct question, numeric result, or case suspense.
2. Format: talking head, real case, two-person dialogue, multi-person scene, whiteboard calculation, or trending story.
3. Emotional driver: financial loss, loss of control, betrayal, invalid agreement, unfairness, or urgent exit.
4. Structure: conflict-consequence-cause-solution, case-turn-legal judgment, or misconception-rebuttal-checklist.
5. Professional density: how much legal explanation appears before retention is likely to fall.
6. Reproducibility: whether success depends on celebrities, complex acting, or a unique case.

Do not store or reproduce long third-party transcripts in the final Markdown. Preserve short hooks, structural paraphrases, evidence links, and research conclusions.

## Comment Research

Review the first useful page of comments for 15–20 high-value videos and classify:

- personal experience;
- request for a specific solution;
- challenge to the conclusion;
- request for a template or calculation;
- emotional reaction;
- user-to-user debate.

The purpose is to capture native audience language, disagreement triggers, shared experiences, and follow-up topic opportunities. Lead quantity is not a primary Round 2 result.

## Execution Phases

### Phase 1: Topic Expansion

- Search 25–30 short, user-language keywords.
- Fetch up to three pages per keyword.
- Deduplicate against Round 1 and within Round 2.
- Produce 200–250 keyword candidates.

### Phase 2: Author Expansion

- Resolve 10–12 author identities.
- Retrieve and rank their high-performing works.
- Add 100–150 author works.
- Determine whether each model is repeatable or accidental.

### Phase 3: Transcript and Comment Analysis

- Select 50–60 representative transcripts across topic, author, duration, format, and performance tier.
- Review comments for 15–20 high-value videos.
- Include both absolute viral hits and relative breakouts.

### Phase 4: Model Synthesis

Produce:

- topic-spread ranking;
- hook-type ranking;
- content-structure library;
- format comparison;
- benchmark-author analysis;
- lawyer-format suitability;
- five to eight repeatable content models;
- 30–50 launch-test topics.

## Stop Conditions

Stop Round 2 when all conditions hold:

- the deduplicated Round 2 pool contains 250–350 candidates;
- each core theme has 30–50 samples;
- each primary model is validated by at least three independent authors;
- each primary model has at least five high-performing works;
- two consecutive new keywords contribute less than 10% new useful candidates;
- new evidence no longer changes the leading topic and structure rankings.

## Data and Safety Boundaries

- Existing database Runs, Candidates, author records, videos, call logs, and request cache may be used.
- Do not promote research candidates into formal materials as part of this round.
- Do not develop a new search or Markdown workflow.
- Record API failures, search drift, identity ambiguity, and missing metrics instead of silently excluding them.
- Verify current law before reusing any legal conclusion in later original content.
