# Risk Cleanup Playbook

`risk_cleanup` is the only stage that changes copy for platform risk. It is a local safety pass, not a second rewrite.

## Responsibilities

- Replace only exact ultra-high-risk promises or unsupported certainty claims.
- Preserve the source hook, subject, quantity hook, authority posture, emotion, rhythm, and propagation tension.
- Record source-carried and edge terms for observation when they are part of the IP context.

## Core Principle

Do not turn risk cleanup into explanatory cooling. Replace unsafe wording with publishable wording that carries similar force.

## Replacement Scope

- Correct: replace the matched phrase only.
- Wrong: rewrite the surrounding claim into a defensive disclaimer or a different argument.
- If the risky phrase is in the opening, keep the opening mechanism and replace only the offending phrase.
- When the risky expression also carries the source's central attitude, first test whether the risk can be bounded through subject, target, condition, or scene context. Prefer contextual limitation over deleting the stance.
- Remove the stance only when its core meaning remains unsafe after reasonable contextual limitation; do not substitute a neutral process merely because it feels safer.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not change body logic.
- Do not add new explanatory disclaimers.
- Do not generate titles, descriptions, or pinned comments.
