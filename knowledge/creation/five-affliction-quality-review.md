# Five-Affliction Quality Review

Use `贪、瞋、痴、慢、疑` as a creator-side attention and payoff lens.

- `贪`: desired gain or avoided loss.
- `瞋`: objection, violation, anger, or urge to refute.
- `痴`: a mistaken model or seductive shortcut to expose.
- `慢`: identity, status, ability, or knowledge comparison.
- `疑`: a credible and bounded information gap.

## Evidence contract

Review all five dimensions. Each status must be `strong`, `weak`, `absent`, or `artificial`.

- The five-affliction opening scope is exactly spoken sentence one plus sentence two. Sentence three is bridge and cannot supply opening evidence.
- `strong`, `weak`, and `artificial` require an exact `evidence_span` from sentence one or sentence two.
- `strong` requires `body_payoff=true` and an exact `body_payoff_span` from the script.
- `artificial` means the stimulus is fabricated, unrelated, or unsupported by the body; it never counts as active.
- The system derives the active count from `strong` statuses and ignores a submitted count.

Five natural strong drivers are the highest target. A pass requires at least three strong drivers across the first two sentences, at least one strong evidence span in each sentence, no artificial driver, one coherent body contract, and complete payoff for every strong driver. Both sentences must be rated strong and the reviewer must identify at least four distinct topic-relevant high-stimulus keywords or phrases that occur literally within them. A pass with fewer than five strong drivers must explain why forcing the missing drivers would damage truth, coherence, IP fit, or source fidelity.

For adaptation, compare the source opening with the current opening. Preserve every source driver that contributes materially to the proven propagation mechanism unless speaker incompatibility or the current body contract makes it invalid. Never stack unrelated money, threat, superiority, mystery, or question language merely to raise the count.
