# Quality Review Playbook

Quality Review is read-only. It evaluates the latest confirmed Script Writer version and never outputs replacement copy.

## Dimensions

- content task and viewer value;
- source propagation mechanism and fidelity when a source is used;
- IP voice, speaker credibility, and professional alignment;
- opening attention through the five-affliction lens;
- one coherent body contract and complete payoff;
- reasoning continuity, specificity, oral fluency, and useful ending;
- absence of mechanical compression, repetition, abstraction, and internal workflow language.

## Progression And Spoken Directness

Treat constructions such as `越……越……` as claims of progression, not as a shortcut for making a sentence sound forceful.

- First identify the two variables. The first variable must be genuinely gradable, and its increase must plausibly strengthen the second variable.
- Require a visible causal bridge. If the relationship only becomes understandable after the reviewer silently supplies a missing premise, mark it for revision.
- Do not bind several different causes into one progression formula. Desire, fear, identity pressure, and outcome risk should be stated directly when they are separate variables.
- Read the sentence aloud. Prefer a direct audience situation, conflict, question, and judgment when that delivers the information faster and sounds more natural in speech.
- Do not revise every occurrence mechanically. Natural change over time or accumulation, such as `越来越沉默` or `越积越多`, may remain when the progression is real and fluent.
- If the progression exists only to create rhythm, intensity, a five-affliction driver, or an imitation of a viral template, treat it as forced progression and a quality failure. Return a bounded revision request to Script Writer; the reviewer must not supply replacement copy.

## Two-Sentence Opening Gate

The opening attention scope is exactly the first two spoken sentences. Sentence three and everything after it are bridge or body and must not be used as opening evidence.

- Sentence one must identify the target viewer and create an immediate high-stakes reason to stay: desired gain, avoided loss, objection, mistaken model, identity pressure, or a bounded information gap.
- Sentence two must escalate rather than explain neutrally. Add a concrete consequence, sharper conflict, costly mistake, identity comparison, or unanswered decision that the body can fulfil.
- Both sentences must independently contain at least one exact `strong` five-affliction evidence span. A pass still requires at least three organic strong drivers across the two sentences.
- Record at least four distinct, topic-relevant high-stimulus keywords or phrases that appear literally in the first two sentences. Generic filler such as `事情`, `问题`, `注意`, or `看看` does not count.
- Do not count a question, list promise, or provocative phrase in sentence three toward opening strength. Sentence three should carry the energy into the case, argument, or promised framework without introducing a new unsupported claim.
- `weak` means the word is present but does not create a meaningful desire, loss, objection, misbelief, identity pressure, or credible information gap. Do not upgrade it because the body later becomes useful.
- If either of the first two sentences is neutral, generic, or merely connective, return `revise` even when the rest of the script is strong.

Do not average away a hard failure. A strong opening with an unfulfilled body contract fails. A useful body with an artificial or misleading opening fails.

## Decisions

- `pass`: the current version may enter Risk Review.
- `revise`: return a bounded revision request to Script Writer.
- `return_upstream`: the brief or source cannot support a local writing repair.

A non-pass result must state exact problems, revision directions, protected elements, protected literal spans, and the maximum edit boundary. Do not include `body`, `draft_text`, `cleaned_body`, `replacement_copy`, or `suggested_copy`.
