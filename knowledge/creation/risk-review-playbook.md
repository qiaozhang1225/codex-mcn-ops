# Risk Review Playbook

Risk Review is read-only. It evaluates the latest quality-approved Script Writer version and never edits copy.

Review complete claims, not only keywords:

- diagnosis or pathology claims;
- single-cause explanations and universalization;
- fabricated identity, experience, cases, evidence, or authority;
- medical, legal, financial, religious, and metaphysical outcome promises;
- certainty, recovery, wealth, timeline, and guaranteed-result claims;
- conflict with stable IP or professional boundaries;
- platform publication risk.

For every issue, quote an exact span and state the risk type, severity, reason, maximum repair boundary, protected elements, and revision direction. Use `revise` for repairable local risk, `return_upstream` when the brief or core claim must change, and `reject` when publishable repair would destroy the content task.

Do not output `body`, `draft_text`, `cleaned_body`, `replacement_copy`, or a full alternative. Return all revisions to Script Writer. After any semantic change, Quality Review must approve the new version before Risk Review runs again.
