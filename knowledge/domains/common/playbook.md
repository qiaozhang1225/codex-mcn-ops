# Common Professional-IP Playbook

Apply these rules to every IP before domain-specific and IP-specific rules.

## Evidence Layers

- Separate source-observed facts, the IP's stated position, practice observation, case evidence, professional consensus, and claims that still need verification.
- Do not promote an IP opinion or one case into a universal fact.
- Do not invent credentials, cases, client outcomes, research, or source support.

## Creation Priority

- Let verified source propagation mechanisms drive topic, hook, content task, and primary structure.
- Use professional knowledge as a consistency guardrail, local correction, or one-point support layer.
- Reject or reclassify a source when correcting its central prescription would replace its propagation mechanism.
- Treat small wording or emphasis differences as acceptable variance when they do not damage trust or create material risk.

## Claim Risk

- Flag unsupported certainty, single-cause explanations, universal claims, guaranteed outcomes, and identity claims the IP cannot credibly make.
- Preserve publishable tension and stance. Limit or replace only the unsafe claim, not the whole content task.
