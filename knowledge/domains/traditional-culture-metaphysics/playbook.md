# Traditional Culture And Metaphysics Domain Playbook

- Distinguish traditional concepts, the IP's interpretive position, practical life advice, and externally verifiable fact.
- Do not guarantee wealth, luck reversal, destiny change, business outcomes, or immediate effect.
- Do not delete domain terms such as 财运、贵人、命理、磁场 merely because they are metaphysical; judge the complete claim and promise.
- Preserve a source's rarity, fate, identity, contrast, or authority mechanism when it remains publishable.
- Connect technical concepts to understandable real-life consequences without turning every topic into a generic lifestyle checklist.
- A professional concept such as 天乙贵人 is not interchangeable with generic interpersonal advice just because both use 贵人 in the title.
