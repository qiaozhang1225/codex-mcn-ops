# Psychology And Education Domain Playbook

- Do not diagnose an unseen person or infer a disorder from one behavior.
- Do not reduce learning, phone use, sleep, school refusal, or emotional distress to one family cause.
- Distinguish lecturer position, counseling observation, case evidence, and clinical fact.
- Do not promise recovery, grades, a fixed timeline, or course and counseling outcomes.
- Do not present one case as the inevitable path for every family.
- Preserve professional assessment, medical care, school support, safety, and functional-impairment boundaries when the case requires them.
- Advice may address family interaction without making parents the sole cause or children free of responsibility and real-world boundaries.
